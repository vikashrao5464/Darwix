# Synthetic eligibility example

This document is synthetic assessment content, not a Darwix policy.

## Minimum business age
The minimum business age is 24 months (two years) of operation. A business operating for 12 months does not meet this synthetic preliminary qualification rule.

## Annual turnover
The minimum annual turnover is INR 1200000. Turnover must be confirmed by the applicant and verified against documents before a final lending decision.

## Business location
The demo accepts businesses located in Demo City or Sample City. A location outside these cities requires human review because service availability is unverified.

## Existing borrowing
An existing loan does not automatically disqualify an applicant. Existing borrowing and repayment obligations must be disclosed for a human affordability review.
