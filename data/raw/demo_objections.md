# Synthetic objection handling examples

## Already have an existing loan
An existing loan does not automatically disqualify an applicant. Explain that existing borrowing must be disclosed and that a human will review affordability before a lending decision.

## Do not want to share documents
If a customer does not want to share documents, acknowledge the concern. Explain that required documents support verification and offer a human callback. Never ask the customer to send identity or bank documents over an unverified channel.
